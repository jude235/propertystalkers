Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Property Stalkers (propertystalkers.com and the Property Stalkers app) is a map of properties for sale, for rent, in co-living and as holiday homes, and of what people are looking for. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address or mobile number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your profile: your name, phone number, your role (owner, broker, developer, buyer or tenant, co-living or homestay host), a broker licence or RERA number if you give one, your interests if you choose them, and a profile picture if you add one.
  • What you post: listings and requests, including location, price, size, description and photos. The unit number you give is kept private and is never shown to visitors; it is only used to stop the same unit being listed too many times.
  • What you do on the site: listings you shortlist or hide, saved searches and alerts, enquiries and viewing requests, and messages you write to our assistant, Stalker, or when posting.
  • On your device: we keep a few settings in your browser's storage (for example your colour theme, map position, chosen filters, and a shortlist if you are not signed in). These are not used for advertising.

How we use it

  • To show your listings and requests on the map, and let interested people contact you by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To match listings and requests, send you alerts you ask for, and show notifications.
  • To check listings for problems such as duplicates or prices that look wrong.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.

What other people can see

When you post a listing or a request, the details you post are public, together with the contact name and phone number on it, so people can reach you. Requests from buyers and tenants are shown to licensed brokers (and co-living requests to co-living hosts). Your email address and unit numbers are not shown to visitors.

Services we use

  • Supabase: stores accounts, listings and photos.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and delivers pages and photos quickly.
  • Google: maps and place search (Google Maps), and sign-in with Google if you choose it.
  • Anthropic: the AI that helps write listings and powers Stalker. The messages and photos you send in those chats are sent to it to produce a reply.
  • Brevo: sends notification emails, when email alerts are switched on.

Each of these handles data under its own privacy terms and only for the purpose of providing its service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and posts while your account is active. You can remove a listing at any time. Listings that are no longer available are taken off the map.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and your posts in My profile.
  • You can clear your hidden listings and shortlist at any time.
  • To have your account and data deleted, or to get a copy of it, email us at the address below and we will do it.

Children

Property Stalkers is not meant for people under 18.
