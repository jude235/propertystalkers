Property Stalkers in Claude

Ask Claude about homes, new projects and asking prices on Property Stalkers, and, if you connect your account, manage your listings and see buyer and tenant requests just by chatting.

For example: "2 bedroom flats to rent in Dubai Marina under AED 150K", "Emaar projects handing over by 2028 under AED 3M", "what do 3 BR villas ask in Dubai Hills?", "drop the price of my Creek Bay listing to 3.1M".

Add it to Claude

  1. In Claude, open Customize › Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Property Stalkers and enter this address: https://propertystalkers.com/mcp
  3. Choose Sign in when needed (searching works without an account), or Sign in now to connect your account straight away.
  4. In a chat, turn the connector on from the + menu and ask away.

When Claude needs your account, it opens a Property Stalkers page where you sign in as usual (Google or an emailed code) and tap Allow. Claude never sees your password.

What Claude can do

Without an accountSearch listings and new projects, open a listing's details and photos, and get asking-price summaries for an area. Phone numbers are not shared.
With your account connectedSee your profile, posts and shortlist; get a poster's phone number when you ask; see buyer and tenant requests if you're a licensed broker (co-living hosts see room requests); draft, publish, edit and remove your own listings and requests.

Claude always shows you a draft and asks before publishing, changing or removing anything. The site's usual rules apply: up to 10 new posts a day, the same unit listed at most 3 times, and you can only change your own posts. Photos are added on the site.

Limits

To keep listings from being copied in bulk, Claude can make up to 60 requests an hour without an account and 300 an hour with one, look up up to 20 phone numbers an hour, and make up to 30 changes an hour.

Disconnect

Remove the connector in Claude, or see and disconnect connected apps at propertystalkers.com/authorize. Connections also end on their own if they're not used for 60 days.

Privacy

When you use the connector, the results of what you ask for (listings, and with your account: your profile, posts and any phone numbers you request) are sent to Claude, which Anthropic runs under its own privacy terms. We keep a record of which apps you connected and short-lived, scrambled sign-in keys, and count requests per hour to apply the limits. See our privacy policy.
